## Why this regeneration was needed

members against **2019** (mid-year stamps equidistant from 2020-01-01; the tie resolved to the earlier index).
Rev 2.1 of the manuscript recomputed every ensemble-wide number from exact-2020 fields (Iceland, RHIME-only,
was never affected). The on-file 06-09 report still carried the pre-fix numbers, so the paper read newer than
its evidence record. This report closes that gap: the numbers below are what the current code produces, and

> **Basis note (resolves the "26 → 59" question raised in review).** The superseded 06-09 report quoted
> EDGAR enrichment **26.0** from `holistic_significance.py`'s built-in strict-`<` ranking (zero cells ranked
> *below* the block). The manuscript and this report use `enrichment_v2.py`'s **midrank** binning (zero cells
> at the *middle* of their tie block, ≈41st percentile), which raises EDGAR's mean to **59.3**. This is a tie-
> handling choice, not new data; midrank is the defensible, less-favourable-to-our-thesis convention and is
> what the manuscript reports. The strict-rank value (posterior 61.9 / EDGAR 26.0) is retained here only as
> the superseded sub-metric.

CF4 is the structural opposite of SF6: the smelter prior **beats** the population proxy where the inversion
can discriminate (Iceland, significant) and directionally over the pooled domain, **loses** in weakly
constrained / coastal-complex domains (Norway, Spain, UK, France-significant), and the decisive China domain
remains untestable from public data. Qualified GO, Europe-validated, China-pending — now with every published
number traceable to this dated re-run.
